WHAT IS FDE?

From Software Engineer to Forward Deployed Engineer

A Forward Deployed Engineer works close to the customer — turning a real business problem into a working, deployed AI solution. Responsibilities vary by company; this is the broader shape of the role.

CODE→INTELLIGENCE→SOLUTION→REAL-WORLD IMPACT

</> Software engineering often involves

  • Building and maintaining applications
  • Implementing technical requirements
  • Developing APIs and software features
  • Testing and maintaining systems

🚀 Forward Deployed Engineering can involve

  • Understanding real-world business problems
  • Working closely with users and stakeholders
  • Building AI-powered applications
  • Integrating models, agents, APIs and enterprise systems
  • Prototyping, deploying and improving practical solutions
  • Connecting engineering work to customer outcomes
